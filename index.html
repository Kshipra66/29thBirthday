The Premise

Somebody Is Lying

Hidden among the group will be Traitors. Everyone else begins as an Innocent. The Traitors know who they are. The Innocents do not. Your job is simple in theory: find them before they remove you from the game.

Your friendships, poker faces and ability to defend extremely suspicious behaviour will all be tested. Alliances are encouraged. Certainty is not.

House Rules

Read Before You Enter

Your role is yours alone. Once drawn, do not show it to anyone.

Trust is a strategy, not a requirement. Lie, accuse, defend and investigate as needed.

Dead players tell no tales. Once removed from the game, you may enjoy the party but not influence the living.

Nothing is accidental. A strange note, missing photograph or suspicious object may mean more than it appears.

At the Circle, speak. Every vote should come with a name, a reason and ideally some unnecessary drama.

The Evening

Expect the Unexpected

The evening will move through games, accusations, private missions, secret decisions and the occasional very public betrayal. Some victories will protect you. Some will place a target on your back. Not every threat will announce itself.

There may be hidden Shields somewhere inside the villa. There may be powers worth fighting for. There will definitely be people confidently accusing the wrong person.
